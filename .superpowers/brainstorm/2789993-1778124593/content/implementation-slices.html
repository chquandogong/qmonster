What the first version should and should not claim
Claim

Situation frequency: exact from persisted recommendations.

Action outcome: exact when an audit or outcome row exists.

Ignored: TTL-based, clearly labeled as Qmonster classification.

Token/cost trend: measured from provider/status-derived samples.

Do Not Claim

No exact "tokens saved" unless provider exposes the counter.

No per-subagent attribution until providers expose structured data.

No auto /compact or /clear beyond existing manual approval policy.

No hidden provider setup writes; Provider Setup remains copy/manual.
